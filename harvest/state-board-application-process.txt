** Mission: 
** The FBLA State Officer Program is dedicated to serving student members by fostering a sense of community, promoting inclusivity, upholding integrity, and instilling a strong sense of professionalism. Our officers serve as ambassadors to advisers, alumni, and the business community, representing FBLA at the local, state, and national levels. The State Officer Program strives to enhance the officers’ ability to contribute to the growth and success of our members.
