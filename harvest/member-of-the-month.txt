## ​Member of the Month ** 

## About ** 

Each month the RI FBLA executive board chooses one exemplary member from across the state to be recognized on our social platforms. You must be a current FBLA member to qualify. To be selected you must be nominated by a fellow member, advisor, or even yourself! More information about member of the month will be released soon!
