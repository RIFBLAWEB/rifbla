## Champion Chapter ** 

**What is Champion Chapter?** 
**The Champion Chapter program highlights chapters that actively engage in a variety of leadership, service, and business-related activities, such as organizing community service projects, hosting workshops, and competing in state and national business events. Through these initiatives, members develop practical skills in areas like entrepreneurship, marketing, and financial management, while also enhancing teamwork and communication abilities. Champion Chapters are recognized for their consistent efforts with awards, certificates, and the opportunity to showcase their accomplishments at FBLA conferences and competitions.
