** ​What is FBLA? ** 

** Future Business Leaders of America (FBLA) is a student organization that prepares members for careers in business and leadership through academic competitions, community service, and networking opportunities. Its mission is to inspire and prepare students to become community-minded business leaders in a global society. FBLA’s purpose is to promote leadership, confidence, and career readiness by combining classroom learning with real-world experiences. Members benefit from leadership conferences, competitive events, scholarships, and skill-building activities that help them succeed in business and beyond. **
